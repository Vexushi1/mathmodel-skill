  - Load task_taxonomy only for classification/modeling/code-analysis routes that actually use objective/structure packs.

  - Load global_preprocessing_contract only when the route decides, executes or inherits preprocessing semantics.

  - Load writing_reasoning_contract for model-design, proposition, algorithm-presentation, non-CUMCM writing fallback and final-review routes that create, revise or formally inspect complex mathematical semantics; ordinary CUMCM LaTeX writing uses the compact writing runtime and loads the full authority only when a declared fallback trigger occurs.
